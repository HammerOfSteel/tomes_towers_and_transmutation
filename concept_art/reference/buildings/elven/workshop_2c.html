Description

This simple Elven Home is a 1-story building with detailed wood and stonework. The set is part of the Noldareth elven collection. The inside is open, able to hold a fair number of minis at once. The house is made to work with any se, but is stylized for the elven collections. 

The Kingdom of Noldareth is an ancient, beautiful land of magic and wisdom, one of the oldest remaining elven kingdoms. The kingdom of Noldareth is tucked away deep into the forest; tall towers and pristine craftsmanship define these halls and buildings. As this age comes to a close, one can still find peace and rest in the land of Noldareth.

- Please Note: The 28mm scale is "true scale" 28mm. All of the doorways are wide enough for 25mm bases to fit through and is intended for play with 25-28mm scaled high-fantasy central earth miniatures. 

What's Included:

1 Elven Home in 2 pieces. 

In 32mm scale, the Library measures 200x156x156mm / 7.9x6.2x6.2 inches    
In 28mm scale, the Library measures 175x137x137mm / 6.9x5.4x5.4 inches    
In 20mm scale, the Library measures 123x96x96mm / 4.8x3.8x3.8 inches    
In 15mm scale, the Library measures 105x82x82mm / 4.1x3.2x3.2 inches    

All items come unpainted in non-toxic gray PLA. Some assembly may be required. We inspect all of our products prior to shipment, but due to the nature of 3d printing, some objects may require some cleanup prior to use.  This item is designed by Conquest Creations and sold under commercial license.
