Free Elf House 3D Low Poly Models

Description

Introducing Free Elf House 3D Low Poly Models. This is a collection of buildings created for fairy folk (Elves are fairy folk in Germanic-Scandinavian and Celtic folklore). The models will be useful for those who create fantasy video games. In addition to houses, the set also contains statues, fountains, awnings, lanterns, etc. A total of 20 objects in low poly style.

Please pay attention! The first preview shows mountains, water and vegetation. All this is not included in the set. The scene is designed for general understanding.

All models use one simple texture atlas, which you can easily modify to change color.

Texture resolution: 64×64, texel density 128 px/m.
The archive contains 3D models in FBX format and textures in PNG format.
