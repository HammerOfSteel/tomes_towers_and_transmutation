# 04 — Transmutation, Alchemy, and Spellcrafting

## Purpose

This document formalizes the **Transmutation** pillar.

The project already contains hints of:
- alchemical floors
- potion brewing
- reagent quests
- spell mutation ideas
- Solmor’s research archives
- loot and gathering ambitions

What is missing is a single design that explains how these become one unified system.

This document defines transmutation as the game’s **great conversion layer**:
the process by which the princess turns raw magical matter, knowledge, and captured phenomena into new forms of power.

---

## Core Fantasy

Transmutation is not “crafting with a wizard skin.”

It is:

- understanding magical matter
- refining unstable loot into useful forms
- developing repeatable magical processes
- mutating spells through theory and catalysts
- solving practical tower problems through alchemical work
- deciding which risks are worth formalizing into doctrine

The player should feel like:

**“I am not just finding power. I am manufacturing it.”**

---

## The Three Faces of Transmutation

Transmutation should unify three related but distinct activities.

## 1. Alchemy
Turn gathered or looted ingredients into:
- potions
- oils
- tinctures
- bombs
- stabilizers
- antidotes
- defense consumables

This is the most immediate and consumable-facing layer.

## 2. Research
Turn books, monster data, and rare materials into:
- unlocked formulas
- new process families
- advanced catalysts
- spell mutation branches
- tower restoration methods
- threat-specific defensive doctrines

This is the longer-horizon intellectual layer.

## 3. Spellcrafting
Turn known spells plus catalysts plus theory into:
- modified spell behaviors
- doctrine choices
- utility variants
- defense-linked spell forms
- risk/reward specializations

This is the build-expression layer.

These should feel separate in use, but deeply linked in inputs and outputs.

---

## Core System Statement

The transmutation loop should be:

```text
[Acquire ingredients, residues, parts, and theory]
    ↓
[Analyze or refine them in the tower]
    ↓
[Unlock formulas, catalysts, or research branches]
    ↓
[Craft consumables or mutate spells]
    ↓
[Use those outputs in expeditions or defense]
    ↓
[Recover rarer inputs from harder content]
    ↓
[Repeat with increasing sophistication]
```

## Refinement Tiers

Raw materials should usually not be the final useful objects.

A core transmutation pleasure should be:

**raw → refined → specialized → signature**

## Tier 1 — Raw
Unprocessed field material.
Examples:
- herb bundle
- cracked shard
- slime core
- burnt ash

## Tier 2 — Refined
Processed into a cleaner, stable baseline form.
Examples:
- distilled extract
- powdered crystal
- clarified gel
- purified residue

## Tier 3 — Specialized
Combined into a purpose-built component.
Examples:
- ember catalyst
- ward solvent
- binding resin
- shock phial
- memory suspension medium

## Tier 4 — Signature
Rare, doctrine-defining outputs tied to advanced theory.
Examples:
- living catalyst
- void suspension
- sovereign tincture
- siege-seal concentrate
- grimoire ink of revision

This structure gives long-term depth without requiring hundreds of unrelated item types.

## Research Branch Structure

Research should not be one flat tree.

It should be divided into major branches that correspond to the title pillars and existing game fantasy.

## Branch 1 — Reagent Science
Focus:
- improve yield
- refine purity
- reduce waste
- unlock stronger standard consumables

Player fantasy:
practical magical chemist

## Branch 2 — Monster Theory
Focus:
- understand enemy biology or magical structure
- improve recruitment odds
- unlock beast-targeted formulas
- derive spell mutations from creatures

Player fantasy:
battlefield natural philosopher / monster scholar

## Branch 3 — Ward and Lattice Theory
Focus:
- tower stabilization
- arcane barriers
- anti-siege prep
- room/floor response doctrines

Player fantasy:
fortress mage

## Branch 4 — Volatile Transmutation
Focus:
- high-risk material conversion
- unstable catalysts
- explosive or extreme spell mutation
- huge power with dangerous tradeoffs

Player fantasy:
reckless magical inventor

## Branch 5 — Sovereign Doctrine
Focus:
- follower coordination
- command magic
- rally and field doctrine
- tower governance as magical practice

Player fantasy:
princess becoming ruler of a domain

## Branch 6 — Ascension and Forbidden Studies
Focus:
- Solmor’s long project
- candidate anomalies
- memory-preserving or form-altering theory
- impossible late-game magical transformations

Player fantasy:
walking into knowledge that should not exist

## Spellcrafting Model

Spellcrafting should be **structured and legible**, not limitless freeform editing.

A spell should be modified through a combination of:

- doctrine branch
- catalyst choice
- school knowledge depth
- sometimes a tower facility requirement

## The four mutation axes

### 1. Form
Changes how the spell is delivered.
Examples:
- bolt → beam
- burst → field
- ward → zone
- chain → anchor mark

### 2. Element / Affinity
Changes thematic or damage nature.
Examples:
- flame → ash
- arcane → glass
- lightning → stormbite
- growth → rot

### 3. Function
Changes practical purpose.
Examples:
- pure damage → mark target
- mobility → rescue follower
- AOE → corridor denial
- pull → gather enemies for defenders

### 4. Doctrine
Adds a strategic identity layer.
Examples:
- expedition doctrine
- dueling doctrine
- recruit-capture doctrine
- defense doctrine
- unstable overclock doctrine

The player should generally choose among curated branches, not arbitrary sliders.

## Example Spell Mutation Paths

## Arcane Ward
Base:
brief invulnerability bubble

### Study outcomes
- lower cooldown
- longer duration
- impact ripple damage
- improved tower barrier synergy

### Mutation paths
- **Mirror Ward** — reflects one projectile
- **Shepherd Ward** — nearby followers gain the ward briefly
- **Anchor Ward** — creates a temporary defensive zone on the ground
- **Glass Ward** — stronger protection, shatters into damaging shards when broken

## Chain Lightning
Base:
hits primary target and arcs to others

### Study outcomes
- more consistent chaining
- anti-automaton bonus
- stun chance increase

### Mutation paths
- **Conductor’s Lash** — fewer targets, more damage per jump
- **Storm Net** — creates a lingering chain field
- **Signal Arc** — marked enemies are prioritized by ranged followers
- **Siege Arc** — weaker combat arc, stronger against summoned waves and swarm units

## Gravity Well
Base:
pulling AOE field

### Study outcomes
- wider pull
- better slow
- combo reveals for explosive alchemy

### Mutation paths
- **Crushing Well** — stronger damage, shorter duration
- **Harvest Well** — pulls drops and loose materials too
- **Sanctum Well** — inside tower defense, creates defender-friendly kill zones
- **Null Well** — suppresses some magical enemy abilities instead of pure pull

## Companion and Transmutation Link

Transmutation should heavily support the companion system.

## Companion-related formula types

### Taming aids
- reduce recruit escape chance
- increase spare window
- calm beast-type enemies
- help capture specific elite archetypes

### Field enhancements
- damage buff tonics
- resistances
- role-specific bonuses for tanks / ranged / swarmers

### Recovery formulas
- revive wounded followers after defense
- restore morale
- clear corruption or instability

### Behavioral doctrine brews
Not direct AI scripting, but a thematic way to empower roles:
- hold-the-line tonic
- pursuit essence
- guard-focus extract
- scavenger draught

This is important because it makes alchemy useful to non-solo builds.

## Transmutation and Quests

Transmutation should solve quests in meaningful ways.

Quest design should often include options such as:
- craft a disruption reagent
- produce a stabilizing tonic
- identify an unknown residue
- create a ritual solvent
- use research to interpret a hidden archive
- prepare a defense formula before a siege beat

### Why this matters
It lets the player feel smart and prepared, not merely strong.

It also ties quests directly into reclaimed tower spaces.

---

## Transmutation and Defense

Defense events should be prepared through transmutation, not tower turret placement.

Before a defense, the player may choose to prepare:

- anti-swarm smoke
- automaton-corroding salts
- rally draughts for companions
- corridor denial resin
- emergency barrier phials
- lure scents for beast manipulation
- recovery stock for attrition defense

### Design goal
A successful defense should partly feel earned **before** it begins because the player prepared correctly.

---

## Risk, Instability, and Forbidden Work

Advanced transmutation should have danger.

Not every branch needs punishment, but the strongest branches should feel volatile.

Possible costs:
- unstable catalyst chance
- self-damage or backlash
- tower contamination event risk
- increased threat generation
- moral/story consequences
- follower corruption risk in certain routes

### Why
Without risk, forbidden knowledge loses tone.
With controlled risk, the player feels they are crossing lines intentionally.

---

## Inventory and Complexity Guardrails

To keep the system manageable:

## Rule 1 — Ingredient categories first
Display ingredients by category before showing every individual item.

## Rule 2 — Most recipes reuse families
Many recipes should share a small set of base refined materials.

## Rule 3 — Unique signature materials should be rare
Exotic items should be important, not constant clutter.

## Rule 4 — Research should reveal relevance
An ingredient should gradually become more legible as the player studies it.

## Rule 5 — Spell mutation branches must stay readable
The player should understand what changed and why.

---

## Implementation Implications

This design implies the following production needs:

1. **Typed reagent families**
   Materials need meaningful categories.

2. **Refinement chain support**
   Raw and refined item states need system support.

3. **Tower-facility-gated crafting**
   Certain outputs require certain reclaimed spaces.

4. **Research project data**
   Books and materials must unlock projects, not only recipes.

5. **Structured spell mutation model**
   Spells need branchable metadata for mutation states.

6. **Companion enhancement support**
   Alchemy should feed follower and defense systems.

7. **Threat-linked preparation**
   Defense preparation should use formulas as strategic tools.

---

## Final Rule

Transmutation content is successful only if it connects at least two of the following:

- a book
- a material
- a tower facility
- a spell
- a companion
- a quest problem
- a defense preparation problem

If it connects only one thing to itself, it is probably just flavor crafting, not a pillar mechanic.
